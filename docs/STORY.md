# Normal Combat — Story & Characters

## Concept
A Mortal Kombat–style 1v1 arcade fighter starring **real, normal, everyday people** — not costumes or caricatures.

- **Art style:** pixel art / retro
- **Tone:** comedy, cartoon violence (no gore)
- **Naming rule:** first names only — no last names for any fighter
- **Animation rule:** 16-bit pixel art — big body gestures, no subtle facial expressions

## Premise: Community Resolution Day
Every year, the neighborhood HOA holds **Community Resolution Day** — a fighting tournament between residents.

- **The prize:** the **Ultimate Neighborhood Wish** — the winner gets one wish granted by the HOA, anything within the neighborhood.
- Each fighter's motive = their wish. Each fighter's ending = what happens when that wish is granted.
- **Final boss:** the HOA President.

## Finisher System
| Moment | Text |
|---|---|
| Prompt before a finisher | **FINAL NOTICE!** |
| Brutal finisher (cartoon) | **VIOLATION!** |
| Nice finisher | **NEIGHBORLY!** |

All finishers must work on every stage (no stage-specific props).

## Fighter #1: Alan

### Bio
- **Age:** 45
- **Who he is:** a regular, middle-aged guy with zero fighting skills. His pride and joy — the love of his life — is his lawn.
- **Fighting style:** untrained, sloppy, desperate. Fights with what he knows: lawn tools.

### Look
- White guy, 45, slightly balding
- Green T-shirt, khaki shorts, canvas shoes
- Panama-style straw hat (no logo/brand) and sunglasses
- **Signature color:** lawn green

### Special Moves
| Move | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Hose Lasso** | Long | Whips the garden hose across the screen, wraps the opponent, yanks them in. Catchphrase: **"GET OFF MY LAWN!"** |
| **Weed and Feed** | Mid | Cranks a handheld spreader, firing a spray of granules. Small damage over time ("weed"); granules on the ground give Alan a small heal ("feed"). |
| **Rake Swing** | Close | Wide, heavy rake swing that knocks down. Slow recovery if it misses. |

Plus shared basics: high/low punch, high/low kick, block, jump, crouch, sweep, uppercut.

### Finishers
- **VIOLATION! — "Sod Off":** Alan rolls the opponent up in a strip of sod, slaps a **"BULK PICKUP"** sticker on the roll, and dusts off his hands while a garbage truck's backup beep plays off-screen.
- **NEIGHBORLY! — "The Gift":** Alan hands the opponent a bag of his premium grass seed, pats their shoulder — *"For your yard."* — and wipes away a single tear.

### Wish & Ending
- **Wish:** to spread his lawn across the entire neighborhood.
- **Ending:** The HOA grants it. Overnight, every yard, fence line, and even the clubhouse floor becomes one endless, perfect Alan lawn. He stands in the middle of it at sunrise, finally at peace... then looks down at his push mower. Then out at hundreds of acres of grass.

### Stats (1–5)
| Speed | Power | Range | Defense | Health |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 3 |

**Playstyle:** keep-away. Strong at distance (Hose Lasso, Weed and Feed), vulnerable up close. Beginner-friendly.

### Intro — "The Inspection"
Alan crouches and pats the ground.
- **On his lawn:** stands and points at the opponent's feet.
- **Any other stage:** stands and throws both hands in the air.

### Win Pose — "The Rake Plant"
Stabs the rake into the ground, leans on it, points at the camera: **"Now THAT'S curb appeal."**

### Taunt — "The Shoo"
Waves both hands at the opponent like chasing a dog off the lawn: **"Shoo! Shoo!"**

### Home Stage
The front of Alan's house and his lawn.
